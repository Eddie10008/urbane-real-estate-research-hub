History & Origin of Urbane Real Estate

From Century 21 franchise origins to an independent, vertically integrated property empire serving Western Sydney since 2017.

Franchise Origins — Century 21 Tan Brothers

Before launching Urbane, Bishal Pokhrel operated as Sales Director at Century 21 Tan Brothers (32 Flushcombe Road, Blacktown) during late 2022 and early 2023, processing listings in Ingleburn and Kingswood. In 2023, both directors broke from the Century 21 network to capture direct equity, avoid franchise royalties, and build an independent brand.

Founder Career Paths

Umang Pokharel accumulated 12+ years of industry experience. His career progression:

  1. Property Management — foundational experience in tenant relations and landlord services
  2. Off-the-Plan Sales — new development and project marketing
  3. Existing Property Sales — established residential market transactions

This trajectory gave leadership deep operational knowledge across every stage of a property transaction — a key differentiator cited in Umang's agent profile.

Historical Context

Urbane launched during a pivotal period for Western Sydney real estate. The 2020–2022 COVID boom, followed by 2022–2023 rate rises, and 2024–2026 recovery created volatile but opportunity-rich conditions. Independent agencies like Urbane benefited from:
  • Migration-driven demand in Blacktown LGA (one of Australia's most diverse LGAs)
  • Greenfield development in Marsden Park and Western Sydney Aerotropolis corridors
  • Investor activity in affordable Western Sydney suburbs ($500K–$900K range)
