NCAT (NSW Civil & Administrative Tribunal)

NCAT handles residential tenancy disputes, strata matters, and property industry complaints. Research findings:

No publicly indexed NCAT decisions were found naming "Urbane Real Estate Pty Ltd" or its principals in open web searches. This does not guarantee zero tribunal involvement — individual tenancy matters are often not published with agency names indexed by search engines.

As a property manager with 116+ annual leases, Urbane likely encounters routine tenancy matters (repairs, bond disputes, rent arrears) handled through standard NCAT processes. These are industry-normal and not necessarily indicative of misconduct.

NSW Fair Trading

All NSW real estate agents must hold a licence under the Property and Stock Agents Act 2002. Complaints can be lodged via:

  • Online: NSW Fair Trading
  • Phone: 13 32 20
No publicly listed disciplinary actions, licence suspensions, or named complaints against Urbane Real Estate or its directors were found in open web research. Fair Trading complaint outcomes are generally not published unless they result in formal disciplinary action.

Research Scope & Limitations

Dark/Deep Web: Not accessed. Such sources are not legitimate channels for business due diligence and may contain unverified or illegal content. No ethical research methodology requires dark web access for Australian business intelligence.

Consumer Protection Framework

Urbane operates under standard NSW real estate regulation including:

  • Property and Stock Agents Act 2002 (licensing)
  • Residential Tenancies Act 2010 (rental management)

  • Privacy Act 1988 (client data handling)
  • Trust account requirements for rental bonds and deposits
