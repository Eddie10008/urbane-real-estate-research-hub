How Urbane Real Estate Operates

Business model, sales process, marketing approach, and technology infrastructure.

Operating Model

  1. Lead Generation — Free appraisal requests, portal listings (Domain, Realestate.com.au, Realty.com.au), Google presence, and agent personal branding.
  2. Listing & Marketing — Professional photography, portal syndication via EagleAgent CRM, signage, and social media promotion.
  3. Sales Process — Agent-led negotiations, open homes, auction/private treaty, contract exchange through external conveyancers.
  4. Property Management — Dedicated rentals team handles tenant acquisition, inspections, maintenance, and lease renewals.
  5. Commission Collection — Standard NSW real estate commission (typically 1.5–3% + GST on sales; 5–8% of weekly rent for management).
