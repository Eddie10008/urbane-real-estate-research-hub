Business Scope

Unlike franchise networks (Ray White, LJ Hooker) or national corporates (McGrath), Urbane operates as a single-office independent agency. Research found no evidence of:

  • Additional branch offices or franchise locations
  • Related property development companies under the Urbane brand
  • Separate holding companies or trust structures (publicly indexed)
  • International operations or offshore entities
Note: ASIC shareholder and director details for private companies require a paid company extract. Public web sources confirm director titles but not shareholding percentages or related entity networks.

Empire Assessment

Urbane Real Estate is best characterised as a focused, high-performing independent agency rather than a multi-entity business empire. Its "empire" is defined by:

  • 273+ recorded property sales on Realty.com.au
  • 58+ suburb coverage across Greater Sydney
  • ~15-person team across sales, rentals, and management
  • Estimated $40–66M annual sales volume (agent-level Domain data)
  • Strong digital presence (website, portals, 364 Google reviews)
