Corporate Genealogy & Integrated Business Empire

Vertically integrated property development and brokerage structure — from Century 21 franchise origins to an independent corporate ecosystem.

Corporate Evolution

The operational history of Urbane Real Estate is linked to Western Sydney franchise networks, specifically Century 21 Tan Brothers at 32 Flushcombe Road, Blacktown. Co-director Bishal Pokhrel operated as Sales Director during late 2022 and early 2023, processing residential listings in Ingleburn and Kingswood.

In 2023, the founders launched independent Urbane Real Estate to capture direct equity, avoid franchise royalty overrides, and build an independent corporate brand — transitioning from commission-earning agents to equity-holding principals.

Corporate structure (simplified):
Century 21 Tan Brothers (Bishal Pokhrel, Sales Director) → Strategic Transition 2023 → Urbane Real Estate Pty Ltd (co-owned by Umang Pokharel & Bishal Pokhrel) → split into Sales & Rentals arm and Construction & Development arm (Urbane Constructions Pty Ltd).

Through Urbane Constructions, the directors acquire land parcels, execute subdivisions, and build residential townhouses and duplexes. Properties are subsequently listed, marketed, sold off-the-plan, or managed as rental assets through Urbane Real Estate.

Vertical Integration Model

Development Margins

Land acquisition and construction profits captured via Urbane Constructions / Urbane Designer Homes.

Sales Commissions

Retail brokerage arm lists and sells completed and off-the-plan stock.

Rent Roll Assets

Long-term property management fees on rental portfolios across Western Sydney ($580–$900/week range).

Project Marketing

Off-the-plan and new estate marketing in Marsden Park, Grantham Farm, and Schofields corridors.

This structure enables co-owners to capture development margins, builder profits, retail sales commissions, and property management fees within a single, self-sustaining corporate ecosystem.

Operational Divisions
