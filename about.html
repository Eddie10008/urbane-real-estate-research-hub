Our Services

URBANE offers residential sales, project marketing, off-the-plan development sales, property management, and rental leasing across 58+ suburbs in Greater Western Sydney — from medium-density townhouses and units to premium freestanding homes.

Our directors bring 12+ years of industry experience each, with deep knowledge of Western Sydney's growth corridors including Werrington, Riverstone, Austral, Grantham Farm, and Schofields. Co-director Umang Pokharel provides bilingual service in English, Nepali and Hindi.

Through our integrated group including Urbane Designer Homes, we offer one-stop property services — making it easier for our clients to realise their dreams. We welcome you to experience the URBANE difference.

Our Story

Founded by directors Bishal Pokhrel and Umang Pokharel, Urbane Real Estate was built on a vision to serve Western Sydney's diverse, fast-growing communities with local expertise and culturally aligned service. Our team has grown to include sales consultants, associates, and a dedicated rentals department — achieving a 4.9-star Google rating from 364+ reviews.
