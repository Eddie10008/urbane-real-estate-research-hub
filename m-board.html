M-Board Guide

Navigation

  • 2D: Pan (drag), zoom (scroll), rotate (right-drag or Ctrl+drag)
  • 3D: Tilt and orbit like Google Earth — scroll to zoom, drag to pan, right-drag to rotate
  • Split: Compare planning layers (2D) with terrain globe (3D)

Planning Layers

  • Land zoning, FSR, building heights, minimum lot size
  • Heritage items & conservation areas
  • Flood, bushfire, biodiversity & contamination constraints
  • ABS demographics, transport & employment data

Cadastre Grid (Premium)

  • Mosaic basemap: Mecone-style planning grid over satellite
  • Lot click: Full metadata — title, zone, FSR, valuations, DAs
  • Search: Find lots by number, DP, or address
  • Tabs: Overview · Planning · Property · Constraints · DAs
  • Hover: Quick lot ID tooltip on cadastre grid
